## What this public cut is for

- Explain the product and its constraints.
- Show the release and evaluation posture.
- Give prospects and partners a stable place to review the public story.
- Keep the implementation core private until a deliberate disclosure step.

## What you will not find here

- transport internals,
- protocol serializers,
- packetizer details,
- private reference tools,
- customer-specific integration notes.
