# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project is intended to be direct, technical, and respectful. We want
people to ask questions, report issues, and propose improvements without having
to fight the tone of the room.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and specific.
- Focus on the work, evidence, and next steps.
- Keep public discussion professional and on-topic.
- Respect boundaries between public-safe, partner-only, and private material.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, slurs, or personal attacks.
- Deliberately leaking private implementation details into public channels.
- Using public issues to pressure disclosure of private customer information.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussion with hostility.

## Enforcement

If a contribution or issue breaks these expectations, maintainers may ask for a
rewording, close the thread, or move the discussion to a private channel.

## Reporting

For moderation or conduct concerns, use the repository's private contact or
security channel rather than filing a public issue.
